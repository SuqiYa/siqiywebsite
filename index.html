Siqi's Portfolio

Hi, I am Siqi, an aspiring screenwriter who has written half-hour sitcoms to horror short films. From a young age, I always liked to make up stories and enact them in real life using my stuffed animals. I also watched a variety of movies, from sweet romances to dark and disturbing documentaries. This allowed me to have a broad knowledge of films, allowing me to choose and write any genre I like.

Recently, I have started watching game shows like The Great Escape. This game touches on real-life events, detective work, and solving puzzles, allowing the player to find out and experience the whole story. This game show also made me interested in trying an escape room. The elaborate and distinct designs and stories of each episode made this show feel like Mango TV truly respects and has a passion for it. The cases that were shown and solved touch on real-life events, making more people aware that committing crimes ain’t the only solution and there are still many joys in life.

I wanted to take MEDP 150 because I heard we could do web development and make websites that beginners could learn from. Even though my first computer science class was hard, I still like the ability to make my own websites. After taking this class, I also found the power of the Photoshop app, as it's really helpful for cropping objects or people and putting them on different backgrounds or even making the photo blurry in some areas to create a cinematic effect. So after this class, I will keep using the coding skills I learned and try to make more advanced websites in the future.
