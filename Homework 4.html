Homework 4: Photomontage

Picture 1

All of the images were taken from online resources given by the professor. The texture I chose was also from the free texture website professor gave to us. This image is to shed light on the dark of depression, as it has become fairly common for the new generation to have depression during their parts of life. This illness might also be the outcome of the decreasing of living conditions, knowledge of what depression looks like, and the pressure on the young to do better and achieve what they could achieve when they were young. I just chopped and cropped out the person and the dark bench and layered on top of the leaves, then I put the dark texture on the back.

Picture 2

The second image was also taken online; it also uses texture from the texture website. I wanted to do something about flowers, as many boyfriends, husbands, or men nowadays don’t really buy their girlfriend, wife, or their close family flowers anymore. Flowers have always been the go-to gift to symbolize love, passion, loyalty, care, apology, grief, celebration, and effort to the receiver. It felt like a loss of remembrance towards our old way of love and connection. Even though flowers are short-lived, when it dies it doesn’t mean the end of love. Instead, it shows people the effort it takes to care for and watch the flower live on, just like how a relationship works. For this, I also cut out the vase and attached it to a background of a bed of flowers. Finally, I added a texture in the middle to make the flowers more rough.
